A little about me

Good work starts with care.

I’m a Nigeria-based designer and developer who likes bringing different disciplines together. I care about the details — how a page feels on a phone, how a cut lands with the music, and how a visual idea stays clear when it meets the real world.

From web experiences to visual storytelling, I work with curiosity, craft and a focus on making the result useful as well as memorable.
